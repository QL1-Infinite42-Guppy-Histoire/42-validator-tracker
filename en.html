About estimated addresses (v0.3.0 / Phase 3): addresses whose link to a validator could not be confirmed by a direct transfer, estimated in three ways — (1) through-pool (a trade on the other side of the same pool matching within 10% in amount and 24 hours in time), (2) 2-hop (validator → this address → validator, both legs confirmed transfers), (3) shared counterparty (a third party transacting directly with 2 or more validators that is not already confirmed-related). Estimated moves appear only in Latest activity, with an Estimated badge and the basis shown. The special sections (Insider / Snipes / Notable / Bridge Withdrawals / Totals) use confirmed links only and never include estimates.

Validators (23)

"N related addresses" is the number of addresses confirmed to have transacted directly with that validator; "M estimated" counts addresses linked only by one of the three estimation methods (no confirmed direct transfer).

Data source: the 42 Chain blocks themselves (via RPC). Tapping a transaction or address opens the official explorer, scan.infinitedrive.xyz. Displayed tokens are mainly the 17 tokens listed on Don't Panic (anything else is marked as a non-listed token). No move is dropped because of its size — small transfers are all recorded. Scope: EVM side + Cosmos side (transfers, delegations, reward claims). Action types are shown with concrete names wherever the destination contract and method identify them (Fluke entry, NFT mint, reward claim, bridge send, and so on); anything that cannot be identified is shown honestly as "Unclassified action (method / destination)". The Bridge Withdrawals section covers only moves that send USDT out to an external chain (bridge sends of other tokens are recorded as regular moves and are not shown in the green section; inbound receipts are ordinary receipts). Estimated addresses (Phase 3): addresses whose link cannot be confirmed by a direct transfer are also watched, estimated in three ways — through-pool, 2-hop, and shared counterparty. Their moves appear only in Latest activity, with a dashed border, an Estimated badge and the basis shown; they are never included in the Insider / Snipes / Notable / Bridge Withdrawals / Totals sections (those stay confirmed-only). The validator list is refreshed from the chain on every run: newly bonded validators are added automatically, and validators that leave the bonded set stay watched as history (marked inactive). Nothing is posted externally (no X bot) — alerts live on this site only. Cosmos-side transaction links open our own Cosmos scanner (42 Cosmos Scan). Headlines show only the address that actually moved; related validators are listed on a separate line, capped (an address that transacts with many validators gets a "Shared counterparty" mark). Validator X is also known as Rioshi, shown in small text as (Rioshi) next to the name.
