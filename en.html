This page automatically collects, from new blocks, the moves of the validators themselves and of their related addresses (everyone who transacted with them, and the counterparties of those counterparties) (EVM side + Cosmos side, v0.4.2). Program (contract) addresses are not counted as a person's relations, and connections are never traced through them. An address is never treated as infrastructure just for being a shared wallet; the only test is whether it is a program (contract). Depth-2+ wallets are counted only when there is evidence they are the validator's own wallet (such as fund movements in both directions). General addresses without evidence are not counted. Moves by addresses whose link is only estimated appear in Latest activity only, clearly marked as estimated (the special sections stay confirmed-only).

📊 Total USDT Bridged Out

How this chart is aggregated: for every validator (all 23), it sums the bridge-outs (USDT) of the validator itself, its depth-1 related wallets, and its depth-2+ related wallets that carry evidence. Depth-2+ wallets are counted only when there is evidence they are the validator's own wallet (such as fund movements in both directions). General addresses without evidence are not counted. Only personal wallets count as senders — infrastructure (program = contract) addresses are never counted, and neither are estimated addresses. Each bar is split into: the validator itself / related wallets that transacted directly with that validator (depth 1) / related wallets connected through other wallets (depth 2+, evidence only). A shared wallet's outflows are counted in the bar of EACH validator it is connected to, so the bars do not add up to the grand total; the deduplicated grand total is shown above.

Latest activity

About estimated addresses (v0.3.0 / Phase 3): addresses whose link to a validator could not be confirmed by a direct transfer, estimated in three ways — (1) through-pool (a trade on the other side of the same pool matching within 10% in amount and 24 hours in time), (2) 2-hop (validator → this address → validator, both legs confirmed transfers), (3) shared counterparty (a third party transacting directly with 2 or more validators that is not already confirmed-related). Estimated moves appear only in Latest activity, with an Estimated badge and the basis shown. The special sections (Insider / Snipes / Notable / Bridge Withdrawals / Totals) use confirmed links only and never include estimates.

Moves marked "Estimated" come from addresses whose direct transfers with a validator could not be confirmed. Full definition: What are estimated relations?

Definition: what "estimated relations" means

An estimated relation is an address we watch because it may be connected to a validator, even though no direct transfer between that address and the validator has been confirmed — so it cannot be stated to be a related party. Estimated relations are kept strictly separate from the confirmed related addresses (those with a confirmed direct transfer). An estimate is only a possibility, never a conclusion.

There are three kinds of basis, and the basis for each address is always written out on that address's cards:

  • (1) Through-pool: the amount a validator received from a pool (a shared place for buying and selling) closely matches an amount that this address had sold into the same pool shortly before — within 24 hours and within 10% in amount. It is unlikely for both the amount and the timing to line up by chance, so the two may effectively be connected through the pool. The basis text shows the amount difference, the time gap, and a score (how close the match is; closer to 1 means more likely).
  • (2) Shared counterparty: a third party that transacts directly with 2 or more validators. It is worth watching because it deals with several validators, but it cannot be concluded to be a "related party" of each of those validators, so it is treated as estimated.
  • (3) 2-hop: an indirect link of the form validator → another address → validator, where both transfers are confirmed. (Currently, no address has been newly registered under this type.)

How estimates are handled: moves by estimated addresses appear only in Latest activity, with a dashed border and an "Estimated" badge, and can be hidden by unchecking "Show estimated" above. They are never included in the Snipe Alerts, Notable, Insider Watch, or Bridge Withdrawals sections, nor in the bridge-out totals chart — all amounts there are totals of confirmed activity only.
