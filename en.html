Validators (23)

"Related addresses" is the number of addresses confirmed to have transacted directly with that validator.

Data source: the 42 Chain blocks themselves (via RPC). Tapping a transaction or address opens the official explorer, scan.infinitedrive.xyz. Displayed tokens are mainly the 17 tokens listed on Don't Panic (anything else is marked as a non-listed token). No move is dropped because of its size — small transfers are all recorded. Scope: EVM side + Cosmos side (transfers, delegations, reward claims). Action types are shown with concrete names wherever the destination contract and method identify them (Fluke entry, NFT mint, reward claim, bridge send, and so on); anything that cannot be identified is shown honestly as "Unclassified action (method / destination)". The Bridge Withdrawals section covers only moves that send USDT out to an external chain (bridge sends of other tokens are recorded as regular moves and are not shown in the green section; inbound receipts are ordinary receipts). Estimated links are Phase 3. Nothing is posted externally (no X bot) — alerts live on this site only. Cosmos-side transaction links open our own Cosmos scanner (42 Cosmos Scan). Headlines show only the address that actually moved; related validators are listed on a separate line, capped (an address that transacts with many validators gets a "Shared counterparty" mark).
Version: v0.2.7 (v0.2.6 + larger name display: validator names are shown bigger on event cards, related-wallet labels and related-validator names slightly bigger too) with count badges linking into each section, plus tap-to-open collapsible sections on the tracker pages to keep the page compact)
